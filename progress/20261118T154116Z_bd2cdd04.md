- Why only after true atoms: checking at every node costs a full closure per node and lost
  eq_diamond6 (>30 s); after true atoms it is 23 s; after true atoms between terms other than TRUE
  (14 s) loses the Rodin/Goel gains, which come from `p = TRUE` atoms: the translation makes every
  occurrence of a symbol a separate term, so occurrences of one Boolean are related only by
  congruence. Skipping Tseitin gate atoms (prototype: 12 s) would need an extra `search`
  parameter; left for the incremental closure step.
