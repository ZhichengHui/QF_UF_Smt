# 2026-11-19, feature session (#60: equality elimination, eq_diamond13..20)

- `check_sat` now runs an equality elimination on the `SmtProblem` before `tr_problem`
  (`eliminate`, end of `core/src/lib.rs`).
  - Rule: a declared constant `c` is eliminated if every occurrence of `c` (in the assertions and
    in the bodies of the definitions) is a side of an equality `(= c g)` or `(= g c)` with a
    constant `g != c` of the same sort, and all these equalities are conjuncts of one `and` at a
    positive position of an assertion (reached from the assertion through `and`/`or` only).
    `c` is then renamed to the partner `g` of the first such conjunct in the assertions.
  - One pass over the symbols in order (`elim_partner`, `renames`). `restore` gives each
    eliminated symbol the table of its partner, in reverse order of elimination.
  - The rename leaves the shape and size of every term unchanged, so the size check on the
    input still bounds the translation of the reduced problem.
- Why the SMT level and not the CNF `Problem`: the Tseitin encoding (`gate_andor`) emits the
  clause `g ∨ ¬(c = t1) ∨ ¬(c = t2)`, so the atoms of `c` occur negatively in the CNF and the
  CNF rule of the issue does not apply to eq_diamond. Over the `SmtProblem` the conjunction is
  visible, and the proof is a substitution lemma plus monotonicity of `and`/`or`.
- Proofs, two new theories in session `QFUF_Top`:
  - `QFUF_ElimSem`: the HOL functions and their meaning. `eval_rename` (renaming `c` to `g` is
    interpreting `c` as `g`), `find_conj_true` (in the conjunction, if true, `c` already has the
    value of `g`; above it only monotone `and`/`or`), `elim_satisfies`, `elim_model`, and the
    loop invariant `elim_inv` with `eliminate_correct`: wf, repr, fragment and `usize_m` are kept;
    a satisfiable problem stays satisfiable; `restore_m` maps a model of the reduced problem to a
    model of the original one.
  - `QFUF_ElimRefine`: the contracts of the Rust functions (`occ`, `eq_partner`, `find_conj`,
    `elim_partner`, `rename`, `eliminate`, `restore`, ...), each `r = <HOL function>`.
  - `QFUF_Top`: `check_sat_spec` composes them; `fuel_top` adds the fuel of the reduced problem
    (`elim_q`). `solve_correct` and `check_sat_correct` keep their statements.
