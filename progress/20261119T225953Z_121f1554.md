## Solver (`core/src/lib.rs`)

- **Watched literals** (deliverable 1). Each clause (problem clauses, then learned ones) has two
  watch slots; the slots that watch one literal form a linked list in flat vectors (`head`,
  `nxt`, `wl`). `watch_pass` propagates from a queue of newly set atoms: `watch_visit` moves the
  slots of the literal made false to another non-false literal, and `units_at` checks the
  remaining clauses with `clause_unit` and sets the unit literals (reason `2 + clause`, level `d`,
  time stamp `s + queue length`). At the root (`s = 0`) every clause is checked once first.
  `cdcl` takes the queue `q` (decision: `[k]`; implied atoms: `new_atoms`; jump: `[u1]`) and
  threads the watch lists through its result; `watch_new` watches each new learned clause on its
  two highest-level literals.
- Soundness needs no property of the watch lists: every atom is set and every conflict found by
  `clause_unit` on the clause itself. Every access to the lists is guarded (`get_or`, `set_at`).
- **Per-symbol candidates in `log_col`** (deliverable 2). `sym_index` sorts the terms by symbol
  once per problem (counting sort, `perm` and `start`); `log_col` compares a term only with terms
  of its symbol. Soundness still rests on `congruent`.
- **Decision order** (`pick`): the unset atom with the highest `act`, where learning clause number
  `m` sets `act` of its atoms to `m` (`bump_last`), i.e. the atoms of the latest conflicts first.
  The issue listed a dynamic order as a follow-up "only if the aim needs it": with cheap
  propagation alone the aim was +1 (PEQ018_size4), with `pick` +4. Its contract is that of
  `first_unset`, so it did not change the proof of the search.
- The front end rejects calls with more than 16 arguments, so `cdcl` takes the symbol index and
  the watch lists (with `act`) as tuples, and a queue instead of two more flag lists. `vec![x]`
  generates stubs of `Box::new_uninit`; `one(x)` replaces it.
- Tried and dropped first: one scan that sets every unit literal it meets (no watches). It halved
  the nodes but each node then scanned all learned clauses (9,000 of 26 literals on PEQ018_size4)
  instead of stopping at the first unit: no faster.

## Proofs

- `proof/QFUF_Watch.thy` (new, between `QFUF_Incremental` and `QFUF_Proof`): `ext_ok p t f t1 f1`
  (`t1`, `f1` extend `t`, `f` by unset atoms, and every model extending `t`, `f` extends `t1`,
  `f1`), with `ext_ok_trans`, `ext_ok_step` (one unit literal, from `find_result`), `ext_ok_fuel`,
  `ext_ok_rep_for`. Contracts: `clause_unit_at` (`find_result`), `units_at` and `watch_pass`
  (`ext_ok` and "a reported clause refutes `t`, `f`"), no-abort contracts for the watch lists,
  `watch_new`, `bump_last`, `new_atoms`, `one`, `sym_index`, and `pick` (the post of
  `first_unset`). `last_idx_lt`/`last_idx_one` moved here from `QFUF_Proof`.
- `QFUF_Incremental`: `log_col` and its callers take `perm`, `st`; the loop over the symbol range
  needs only the guard `j < len(terms)`.
- `QFUF_Analyze`: `unit_index_contract` removed (`unit_index` is gone).
- `QFUF_Proof`: `cdcl_contract` unchanged except a free type for the watch lists in the result;
  four step lemmas for the pass (`step_pass_*`). The rules are applied per goal (`solves`), once
  directly and once to goals `auto` left as `¬ P ⟹ False` (`rotate_tac -1, erule notE`); the old
  `(rule …; assumption)+` chain stopped at the first goal it could not close.
  `solve_correct` and `check_sat_correct` unchanged; `spec/` unchanged.
- `urust/` regenerated: 0 placeholders, templates and locale unchanged, `tools/check-spec.sh` ok.

## Notes for a lessons session

- The Pool Server sessions of a task group answered `busy` for ~15 min while a scratch session
  loaded a theory that was not in the heap (`QFUF_Incremental`, minutes of `crush`). Put every
  finished theory into the task heap before working on the next one.
- A batch check (`isabelle build`) has no time limit per step: one slow step hid for 10 min. Use
  it only for theories that already check in the Pool Server.
- After `auto`, goals can come back as `¬ P ⟹ False`; a rule list applied with
  `(rule …; assumption)+` silently stops at the first goal it cannot close. Apply per goal with
  `all (solves …)` instead.
- `start` is a constant (of `range`); a variable of that name in a lemma statement is a type error.
