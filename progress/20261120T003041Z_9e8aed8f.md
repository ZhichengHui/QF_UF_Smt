# 2026-11-20, feature session (#73: profile-led speed-up of cdcl)

The aim is met: 99 → 100/101 on `QF_UF_Smt-2026-11-18` (+NEQ016_size6, 22.9 s), 0 WRONG, none
lost, and the time on the 99 both solve 15.31 → 14.66 s (−4 %). PEQ018_size5 still times out.

## Profile (main, callgrind and temporary counters)

- Both targets spend most of their time in the closure, not in copying the per-node state
  (memcpy/memset ≈ 5–7 %): the candidate scan of `log_col` (`congruent` and its loop ≈ 28 %), the
  explanations (`path_explain_log` + `rows_join` ≈ 16–20 %), `merge` (≈ 5 %).
- Counters: NEQ016_size6 compared 2.7 G term pairs in 20 s, about 390 per moved row (a row was
  compared with every term of its symbol, 171–182 terms for the large symbols); PEQ018_size5
  2.1 G in < 20 s. `bfs_round` rescanned the whole merge log once per BFS layer.

## Solver (`core/src/lib.rs`)

- **Congruence candidates by first-argument class.** `round_moved` builds `buckets` once per
  round: the terms with arguments, listed per class of their first argument, in increasing term
  order. `log_bucket` compares a moved row only with the terms of its list; constants keep the
  symbol scan (`log_col`). A list can go stale within a round, but a term whose first argument
  changed its class is a row of the next round, so the closure stays closed. Comparisons dropped
  50–900× (NEQ032_size3 85 M → 1.6 M). Soundness still rests on `congruent`.
- The order of the lists matters for the search: lists in decreasing term order (the first
  version) changed the merge order, hence the log, explanations and learned clauses, and lost
  time (NEQ004_size4 2.6 → 51 s). With increasing order the merges are almost those of the old
  scan; the search still differs slightly (NEQ032_size3 3.1 → 4.7 s, others faster).
- **Explanations:** `log_adj` builds adjacency lists of the merge log once per `path_explain_log`;
  `bfs` is a queue BFS over them (`bfs_visit`) instead of rounds over all edges. In a forest the
  path is unique, so the explanations are unchanged. `walk` stops at `a` instead of running `n`
  steps.
- Instructions on NEQ016_size5: 24.5 G → 14.5 G (−41 %). Conflicts/s (20 s, under the load of
  `isabelle-check`): NEQ016_size6 865 → 1,880, PEQ018_size5 1,540 → 1,930.

## Follow-up (PEQ018_size5)

Not reached with constant factors (1.25× conflicts/s). Remaining costs after this change:
`analyze` (≈ 12 %: `last_at`/`other_at` scan all atoms per resolution step), `rows_join` (≈ 13 %:
a closure from scratch per explanation, `merge` O(n)), `merge` O(n) overall (≈ 9 %). Likely
needs search changes: restarts with phase saving, clause minimisation (`cdcl` never restarts and
falls back to `search` when a jump lands without a unit clause).
